## 5. Cross-file reconciliation
- Apps (all apps listed) vs national volume over 20 overlapping months (2022-01 to 2023-08): average difference 1.5%, range 1.1% to 2.0%
- Remitter banks (top 50), approved volume vs national volume over 20 overlapping months (2022-01 to 2023-08): average difference -2.1%, range -2.4% to -1.8%
- Beneficiary banks (top 50), approved volume vs national volume over 20 overlapping months (2022-01 to 2023-08): average difference -1.7%, range -4.0% to 1.3%
- Reading guide: the national file counts successful transactions only, while the bank files' total volume also includes declined ones, so bank files are compared on approved volume. Bank files cover only the top 50 banks, so a gap well below zero means banks outside the top 50 carry meaningful volume. Small positive gaps can come from rounding and from deemed approvals.
